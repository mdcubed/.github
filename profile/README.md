We study how ligands, genetic variants and receptor isoforms change the dynamics and signaling of G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs), and we use what we learn to design ligands. Most projects combine membrane molecular dynamics, free-energy calculations and interpretable machine learning, and run alongside experimental pharmacology and medicinal chemistry collaborators.

### Research

**Mechanism to prediction.** We turn features from simulations (time-resolved contacts, activation switches, helix motions, hydration, allosteric communication) into predictions of ligand selectivity and signaling bias. Current targets are serotonin, dopamine, histamine and incretin receptors, plus an orphan receptor.

**Photoswitchable ligands.** We design and screen ligands whose activity can be switched with light, building on [PSW-Designer](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.3c01050).

**Allosteric modulation.** We model positive and negative allosteric modulators (PAMs and NAMs) of GPCRs and neurotrophin receptors.
